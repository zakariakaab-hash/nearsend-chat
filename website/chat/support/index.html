Nearsend Chat lets you chat with people nearby over Bluetooth, with no internet, no account and no phone number.

Getting started

  • Install Nearsend Chat on two or more phones and open it. Allow Bluetooth when asked.
  • Phones find each other automatically and show up on the Nearby tab. Tap a person to chat.
  • Every extra phone with the app stretches the range: messages hop from phone to phone, up to 5 hops.

Common questions

I can't see anyone nearby. Check that Bluetooth is on, that the other person has the app open or running in the background, and that you are within about 10 to 30 metres. Walls and crowds shorten the range. On iPhone, You → Bluetooth shows how many people are in range and through other phones.

Nearby says "Bluetooth is off". Open Control Center (swipe down from the top-right corner) and tap the Bluetooth button so it turns blue. If Settings → Bluetooth shows Allow New Connections, tap it. Apps cannot turn Bluetooth on themselves.

Someone I don't know sent me a message. On iPhone it arrives as a message request: open it and tap Accept to chat, or Block. You can choose to allow or block all strangers in You → Privacy.

My message says it is waiting. The person is out of range. Phones nearby carry the message for up to 24 hours and hand it over when the person comes back in range.

Are my messages private? Private chats are end-to-end encrypted: phones that pass them on cannot read them. The Nearby channel, announcements and SOS alarms are public and not encrypted. The app has not been security audited yet.

Does SOS call the emergency services? No. An SOS alarm reaches phones nearby that have Nearsend Chat. If you have cell signal, call your local emergency number.

I turned Bluetooth access off by mistake (iPhone). Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Nearsend Chat and switch Bluetooth on. On Android: Settings → Apps → Nearsend Chat → Permissions → Nearby devices.

Can I try the app alone? Yes. On iPhone open You → Bluetooth and turn on Demo mode: it shows sample people and chats. Bluetooth is off while it is on, and your own chats come back when you turn it off.

I have a new phone. How do I keep my chats? On the old iPhone: You → Move to another phone → "This is the old phone", choose a password and make the file, then send it to the new phone (AirDrop or Files). On the new iPhone: install the app, then You → Move to another phone → "This is the new phone", choose the file and type the password. Afterwards erase the old phone: the same identity on two phones confuses delivery.

Can I lock the app? Yes, on iPhone: You → Privacy → App lock. It asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode when you open the app, and notifications no longer show message text.

How do I invite a friend? You → Invite a friend shows a QR code that opens Nearsend Chat on the App Store. Your friend needs internet once to download it; after that it works with no signal. Then add each other with My code.

Does it work when the screen is locked? Yes. The app keeps receiving and passing on messages in the background, a bit more slowly. On iPhone, if you swipe the app away in the app switcher, iOS stops it until you open it again. With low battery (20 % or less), Battery saver or Low Power Mode, the app looks for phones less often, so messages may take a little longer.

How do I delete my data? You → Account → "Erase everything". It deletes all messages, keys and settings on this phone. We never had a copy.

Report a person or content

Long-press the message (or open the person's contact info) and tap Report. This opens an email to support@nearsend.app with the details. You can also Block the person: their messages are then hidden and ignored.
