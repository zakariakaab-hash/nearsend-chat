The mesh forms itself.

Every phone with the app is both a user and a relay. There is nothing to set up beyond choosing a name.

01

Phones find each other

Open the app and allow Bluetooth. People nearby appear on the radar within seconds, usually 10 to 30 metres away.

02

Messages hop

A message can pass through up to 5 phones to reach someone further away. Private messages stay encrypted, so the phones in between cannot read them.

03

Out of range? It waits

Phones nearby carry your message for up to 24 hours and hand it over when the person comes back in range.

What you can do.

  • Private chats, end-to-end encrypted.
  • Group chats and a public Nearby channel for everyone in range.
  • Announcements that stay visible nearby for a few hours.
  • Small photos to a contact in range.
  • Verify a contact by comparing fingerprints in person.
  • Block and report from any message.
  • Choose a name, an emoji and a colour. That is the whole setup.
  • Decide whether your phone passes on messages for others.
  • Turn off delivery receipts or hide message text in notifications.
  • Delete messages automatically after 1, 7 or 30 days.
  • "Erase everything" wipes the app in one tap.
  • 12 languages.

Private by design.

No accounts, no servers of our own, no ads and no analytics. Your messages and keys stay on your phone.

Encrypted: private and group chats

X25519, HKDF and ChaCha20-Poly1305 from Apple's CryptoKit. Phones that relay a message cannot read it.

Public: Nearby channel, announcements and SOS

Anyone in range can read these. The app labels them clearly.

Nothing to collect

There is no sign-up and nothing is sent to us. Read the privacy policy.

SOS

Alert everyone nearby

Hold the SOS button to send an alarm to every phone nearby that has Nearsend Chat, also through other phones. You can add a note and your position. It does not call emergency services. When you have signal, call your local emergency number.

Honest about the limits.

Better you know them now than find out when it matters.

Not security audited

The encryption uses standard building blocks, but nobody independent has reviewed the app yet. Do not rely on it where your safety depends on it.

Short range

Roughly 10 to 30 metres per hop, less through walls and crowds. Messages only travel as far as there are phones to pass them on.

Keep the app running

It works in the background, but if you swipe it away in the iPhone app switcher, iOS stops it until you open it again.

Brand new

Version 1.0 has not been tried on many phones yet. Problems? Tell us at support@nearsend.app.
